# Executive Sales Analysis Report
> 2022 vs 2023 Sales Performance

## Executive Narrative
National sales performance across the first half of 2023 demonstrated stable sales expansion.

Key Findings:
* Uniform Expansion: 100% of reporting provinces recorded positive year-over-year growth in June 2023.
* Sales Volume: Saskatchewan continues to lead national top-line revenue, generating approximately $2.95M in June 2023 and approximately $7.95M in 2023 Q2.
* Seasonal Acceleration: All regions exhibited positive sequential growth from 2023 Q1 to Q2 2023.

### 2022 vs 2023 QTD Sales by Province

Observations:
* Sales performance between June 2022 and June 2023 are near identical across all positives, with June 2023 indicating smaller increase in sales revenue compared to the prior year.
* Saskatchewan and Quebec represent the largest June sales increase compared to the other Provinces, whereas New Brunswick and Ontario represent the smallest sales increase.

![2022 vs 2023 QTD Sales by Province - Chart]()

![2022 vs 2023 QTD Sales by Province - Table]()

Observations:
* British Columbia, Nova Scotia, Ontario, PEI, Quebec, and Saskatchewan experienced a significant increase in sales in Q2 of 2022 & 2023, compared to Q1 of 2022 & 2023.
* New Brunswick followed a similar trend as aforementioned, however, with a much smaller increase in sales. 
* Interestingly, Alberta is the only Province that experienced a decrease in sales in Q2 of 2022 & 2023, compared to Q1 of 2022 & 2023.

## Strategic Recommendations
1. Focus on Growth Investments: Reallocate a portion of budgets toward lesser performing Provinces, such as British Columbia and Quebec, to capitalize on high relative growth velocity.
2. Maintain Key Accounts: Reinforce relationship management in Saskatchewan and  Ontario to safeguard major revenue foundations.
3. Align Planning: Ensure quarterly targets account for historical Q1 to Q2 seasonal velocity shifts observed across all markets.
